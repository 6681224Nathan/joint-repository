2\. Plan for Assessing Course Learning Outcomes  
     2.1 Assessing and Evaluating Learning Achievement  
	a. Formative Assessment  
	The assessment tools such as individual assignment, quizzes and final exam are used to evaluate student’s understanding by their ability to describe computer graphics, geometric transformations, creating two-dimensional objects, two-dimensional viewing, creating three-dimensional objects, color theory, texture mapping, lighting and cameras, lighting and shading, create an effect, and animation. Students should be able to demonstrate how to design and create computer graphics scenes using graphics programming methods given during lectures. The assessments are made through their individual assignment, quizzes and final exam. The computer graphics project has to be shown by applying an understanding of the concepts in computer graphics methods to solve engineering problems. The assessments are made through their group assignment with a project report, discuss on project.

3\. Student Appeals  
	 The student wishing to appeal according to grading result must submit a written and signed appeal form personally to the academic affair unit.
