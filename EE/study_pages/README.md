# EGCI 232 study pages

Open any file in a browser. Each page ends with practice problems and their solutions.

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [S0_circuit_toolkit.html](S0_circuit_toolkit.html) | Circuit basics you need before diodes and BJTs: Thévenin/Norton, mesh vs nodal, how capacitors act in DC and AC, and what the sine-wave numbers (V_p, V_rms, f) mean. |
| [S1_diode_circuits.html](S1_diode_circuits.html) | How to solve circuits with diodes: the diode models, the "assume ON/OFF, then check" method, and finding the Q-point with a load line. |
| [S2_rectifiers.html](S2_rectifiers.html) | How half-wave, center-tapped and bridge rectifiers turn AC into DC, with and without a capacitor, plus the ripple and design formulas. Uses ideal diodes and the professor's circuit layouts, like the lecture notes. |
| [PIV_circuits.html](PIV_circuits.html) | Shows each rectifier at the moment its OFF diode is squeezed hardest, so you can see where PIV (2V_m, 2V_m, V_m) comes from. Uses the professor's bridge layout and ideal diodes. |
| [S3_BJT_amplifier.html](S3_BJT_amplifier.html) | Full BJT tutorial from zero: what a transistor is, how it works, modes (with an interactive Q-point explorer), DC biasing, the CE amplifier, a step-by-step DC analysis of the CE amplifier (section 6b, with a stepper and one example carried through to the gain), the hybrid-pi model, a step-by-step AC-circuit builder, the professor's slide 16 example, and practice P1–P7 plus multiple-choice checks. |
| [Midterm_prep.html](Midterm_prep.html) | What quiz 1 tested and what the midterm probably adds, 16 new theory MCQs, the dual-supply (± supply) CE amplifier, and new rectifier design problems in the quiz style. |
| [S3S4_BJT_amplifier_crash.html](S3S4_BJT_amplifier_crash.html) | 20-minute crash course on the BJT common-emitter amplifier: DC bias (currents and voltages), the load line, the hybrid-pi AC model, and the gain formulas. |
| [Crash_course_quiz1.html](Crash_course_quiz1.html) | One-page quick review of both Quiz 1 topics (rectifiers and the CE amplifier). It was made before the separate pages above, so prefer those. |
