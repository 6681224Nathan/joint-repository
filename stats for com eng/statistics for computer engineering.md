## Hypothesis testing
To measure the expected mean and the real mean
Hypothesis : mean, to hypothesis the result
if the result exceeds the acceptable domain, the hypothesis thus will be rejected

## Errors
Reject null hypothesis (the hypothesised value), when it is true defined as **type I error**

Failing to reject the null hypothesis when it is false is defined as **type II error**

## Area of rejection, the alpha
**Two sides** : bell curve, \alpha/2 on two tails 
**One side** : bell curve, one side, either left or right \alpha on rejected area
